# Responsive review

Validate 1440px, tablet and 390px layouts. Preserve the decision path, readable media crops and
reachable CTA. Require no horizontal overflow, 44px interactive targets, keyboard-accessible
controls and no hover-only action. Scroll-test persistent navigation at the top, midpoint and
near the end of every scrollable page; ensure sticky filters or section controls remain below it.
Inspect the complete footer and verify primary CTA labels are not clipped in any viewport.
