# Creative brief
Inputs: evidence, business brief and media manifest. Outputs: tone, positioning, art-direction constraints, proof requirements and anti-template constraints.
Rules: make one defensible creative proposition; distinguish proof from mood. Prohibited: visualising unverified outcomes as proof.
Failure conditions: generic direction or contradiction with evidence. Checklist: audience, page job, evidence signals, CTA and avoid-list are present. Done when a builder can use it without guessing.
