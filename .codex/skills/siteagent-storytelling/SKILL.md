# Storytelling
Inputs: business brief and UX architecture. Outputs: narrative strategy, emotional curve, storyboard and proof requirements.
Rules: each section answers a customer question and advances the decision. Prohibited: filler transitions, fake social proof or disconnected blocks.
Failure conditions: no opening thesis, progression, or final decision moment. Checklist: purpose, message, question, proof and CTA are recorded for every section. Done when the storyboard is complete.
