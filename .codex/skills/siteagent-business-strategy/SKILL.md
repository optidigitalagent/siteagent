# Business strategy
Inputs: research and evidence assessment. Outputs: business brief, audience, objections, primary/secondary CTA and prohibited claims.
Rules: one clear customer decision; only verified location/offers; favour Direct when details are missing. Prohibited: fabricated differentiation or guarantees.
Failure conditions: missing business identity, goal, CTA, or source link. Checklist: audience, intent, objections, trust opportunities and unavailable information are explicit. Done when the brief validates.
