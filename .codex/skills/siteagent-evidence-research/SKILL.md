# Evidence research
Inputs: Instagram URL, scrape context, resumed artifacts. Outputs: research brief, evidence assessment, prohibited claims and sources.
Rules: label facts with sources; preserve unknowns; use only public real media. Prohibited: invented offers, contacts, prices, people, reviews, locations, or visual claims.
Failure conditions: contradictory identity, no contact path, or evidence Level C. Checklist: facts and assumptions are separate; sources exist; CTA is supported. Done when the evidence artifact validates.
